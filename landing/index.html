Most of a day vanishes because nobody had time to write it down. NeoRecall is built the other way around: it listens with consent, uses inference services you choose, and keeps the useful parts — not the recording.

01

It keeps up

Durable chunks survive weak connections and interrupted days. Every source can record offline and resume safely when the server returns.

02

It understands

Configured transcription and language-model services, combined with local search, turn raw conversation into names, decisions, promises, and moments.

03

It lets go

A terminal receipt proves the transcript is durable and the server copy is deleted before any client is allowed to release its audio.

Keep living. NeoRecall keeps up.

Record from web, desktop, imports, or a wearable. Independently decodable chunks queue locally, upload idempotently, and survive the ordinary failures of an ordinary day.

Understand

One conversation becomes one useful memory.

Your configured transcription and language-model endpoints work with NeoRecall's multilingual retrieval and self-hosted database. Search exact words or ask naturally across people, facts, tasks, and decisions.

Release

Deletion is a protocol, not a promise.

The client holds every audio chunk until a terminal receipt proves transcript persistence and server-side deletion. Only then can it release its own copy.

One memory, many sources

Record where the day actually happens.

Start on the web, keep going on a desktop, import an old recording, or connect a wearable. Every client follows the same durable capture contract, so changing devices does not create a hole in your day.

  • Web, macOS, Windows, and wearable capture
  • Microphone, optional system audio, and file imports

Yours, actually

Your memory should live on your machine.

NeoRecall keeps its database, search index, and durable capture state on your hardware. Speech and language inference always use services you configure separately, whether those are hosted APIs or endpoints on another machine you control.

  • Open source, AGPL-3.0 — inspect every layer
  • Bring any supported provider or compatible endpoint
  • SQLite memory that you can search, back up, or erase
